Riyadh as-Saliheen

A private hadith reader with Arabic and English, search, bookmarks and reading progress.

Source on GitHub →
Context
Our own app
Type
Reading app for web and Android
Stack
React, TypeScript, Vite
Status
Being prepared for Google Play

The problem

Many hadith apps are full of ads, ask for accounts, or mix sources without saying where the text came from. Readers want a calm app that respects the text and their privacy.

What we built

Arabic with EnglishEach hadith shown with its English translation, chapter by chapter.
Search and bookmarksFind a hadith quickly and keep your place.
Reading progressPick up where you stopped, per chapter.
Clear sourcesText from IslamEnc, credited, with original numbering kept.

How it works

The text is imported once from the published IslamEnc edition, validated, and stored as static files the app reads. No account, no tracking. The interface works in English and Urdu, and grades are shown only where the source supplies them.

Status, honestly

The code is public and the app is being prepared for Google Play. This is the kind of bilingual, careful build we do for Islamic organisations.
